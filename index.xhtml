How to have the best order at Chili's

From a true Chili's expert

My qualifications:

  1. Chili's is easily my favorite resturant. I've been to Chili's for my birthday, half-birthday, post-concert chows, theatre meals, dates, breakdowns, and pretty much every other life event.
  2. I am at my hometown Chili's so often the waitresses recognize me, I follow them on Instagram, and we always talk when I go in.
  3. I regularly theme my staff meetings with a Chili's theme, and anyone who has known me for more than a day can attest to my Chili's love.
  4. I essentially have the menu memorized.

While some may believe that eating at Chili's is just another meal, it is actually a gateway to flavor or door to destruction depending on your order. Chili's is one of America's best resturants because of the atmosphere but also their extensive menu. It's important to do research before you go, and understand how to order in a way that will enhance your experience.

1. Timing

Going to Chili's during the early hours of the day (10am-3pm) is a mistake unless you dont want to enjoy your experience. You need to allow yourself to get hungry throughout the day and look forward to the experience. Going too early may spoil the flavor of the food, alongside the quality. I reccomend going to Chili's at 7:30pm, making adjustments for table avalibility. This will allow you to get the full dinner experience wihtout being awake too late.

2. Preperation

If you want to fully enjoy your order, I reccomend eating little throughout the day and skipping apps. While the chips and queso and delicious, they take away from the supeiror food we'll discuss in later sections. If you are looking for leftovers, then go ahead with apps.

3. Ordering Correctly

I would like to preface that the best Chili's experience is the one that you can fully enjoy, so if my reccomendations aren't your preference that is okay. This is simply my advice after going to Chili's dozens of times through my 19 years of life.

Triple Dipper

Arguably one of Chili's most famous dishes, the triple dipper is one of the best fast casual apps/entrees I've ever had. It is truly reminiscent of America with it's huge poriton sizes, emphasis on processed cheese, and delicious sauces. To maximize your triple dipper enjoyment, I reccomend the following combination:

  1. Big Mouth Burger Bites: you get two sliders that offer a nice bread-and-meat note to your meal. they are classic, a fan favorite, and a yummy bite.
  2. Fried Mozzerella: if you've seen Chili's on the internet, you've seen the mozzerella. That cheese pull is to die for, but be sure to dip it into the marinara for flavor, as the stick often comes out greasy.
  3. Honey Chipotle Dipped Crispy Chicken Crispers: This is my most controversial take, but I don't care. I stand by this, as the sauciness from the chicken crispers and hint of spice offers such a contrast from the greasy burgers and mozzerella. This is a CRITICAL STEP if you want to enjoy your experience.

4. Enjoy and Prepare

Now it's time for the easiest step: eating! Make sure to have napkins on hand and lots of extra sauce (many like ranch, but I prefer honey mustard). The preperation comes after the eating. Chili's has greasy, processed, high-calorie food: many patrons get stomach pain after indulging. It's important to pace yourself, not eat at Chili's too frequently, and understand the potential illness you may have after your meal. Enjoy your Chili's and thank you for reading!
